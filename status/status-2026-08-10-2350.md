## Key Decisions & Findings
- **`ble_gap_disc(duration_ms=0)` ≠ forever** — it maps to the 10.24 s
  default window; `BLE_HS_FOREVER` is the continuous-scan constant.
- **Closing the host COM port resets the ESP32-S3** (`ESP_RST_USB`).
  This is the same root cause behind every "scan silently died / counters
  zeroed" observation; light sleep was ruled out. `test_ble.py`'s
  open/close-per-command pattern triggers it — long-running checks must
  keep the port open (`capture_25s.py` kept as the standing tool).
- Silent resets are invisible without diagnostics — `esp_reset_reason()`
  in STATUS/boot banner proved decisive; kept permanently.
