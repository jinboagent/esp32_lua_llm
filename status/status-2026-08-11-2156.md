## Key Decisions
- H3 resolved by documentation, not a buffer bump: the LLM-loop primary path
  (`SCRIPT LOAD` text lines) is unaffected; the hex path now documents its
  real 121-byte/chunk limit. Buffer growth is a v2 candidate (also listed:
  host-only encoder's 2-digit `\u` escapes, test-path only).
- H2 drain is fail-open with a cap: a pathological EOL-less flood >8 KB
  stops the drain rather than wedging the CLI task.
- Peer-suite capture threshold: WinRT exposes no advertising-interval
  control on this adapter, so ≥2 exact-field captures in 8 s is the
  deterministic bar (exact `manu.id`/`manu.data` match carries the proof).
